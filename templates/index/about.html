About

What TopDog is, and what it isn't.

TopDog is a Market Visibility Index (MVI) for regulated online casinos. Every night, it reads the same homepage and live-casino lobby data that licensed operators load for their own players, and turns it into a single, comparable score: which games each operator is actually merchandising, and how prominently.

Placement matters as much as presence. A title sitting in a hero carousel is worth more than the same title buried on page three of a grid, so every tile is weighted by section and on-page position before anything is ranked. Those scores roll up per game, per provider, and per regulated market, so trends - a new release rolling out across operators, a provider gaining shelf space, a market's tastes shifting - show up as they happen rather than months later.

Everything the index runs on comes directly from public-facing pages of licensed operators; nothing here is an invitation to play, and no player-level or account data is collected or published.
